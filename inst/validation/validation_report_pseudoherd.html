Share between categories

To ensure that the pseudoherd is consistent with the observed herd, we compare the share of each livestock category in the total number of animals in the observed herd and in the pseudoherd. We add to the observed herd the number of animals exported, as the pseudoherd is meant to account for all animals involved in the production, including those exported.

# herd
herd_total = object@herd |>
  # add SYS02
  dplyr::left_join(
    object@farm |>
      dplyr::select(dplyr::all_of(id_cols), SYS02),
    by = id_cols
  ) |>
  dplyr::summarise(
    category_count_national = sum(Qobs * SYS02, na.rm = T),
    .by = c(FADN_code_letter, species, YEAR)
  )
